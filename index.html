Axes

Advanced: define quantities and choose the x-axis

Give a name to an expression in the variable, then use that name in any curve — and pick it as that curve’s x-axis. Useful when the quantity you want across the bottom is only known as a function of the variable, such as Z against reduced pressure. With no definitions nothing changes, and the x-axis stays the variable.

While a curve plots against a defined quantity, the “Variable from/to” range above is the sweep range, not the x-axis.

Syntax and built-in constants

Operators: + - * / ^ (or **). Implicit multiplication works: 2x, 3(x+1). Scientific notation: 1.38e-23.

Functions: sin cos tan asin acos atan sinh cosh tanh exp ln log10 log sqrt abs erf floor ceil min max. log is natural log; use log10 for base 10.
